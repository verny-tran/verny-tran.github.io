A dedicated Apple Developer

Presently works as an iOS Engineer at SilverAI JSC in Hanoi, building AI-powered photo and video apps: object removal and retouching, film emulation, real-time rendering and on-device machine learning, in Swift, Metal, Core Image, Accelerate, Core ML and AVFoundation. Before that I was an iOS Engineer at CELEBe Korea and a Research Assistant at the School of Computer Science & Engineering, International University (VNU-HCMC), researching applied AI and mobile vision systems. I have over 10 years of practice developing for iOS and macOS, since sixth grade, when iOS still adhered to the skeuomorphic design language. Beside the coding work, I am also a proactive graphic, branding, and motion designer.
