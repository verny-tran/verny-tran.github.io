Overview

CELEBe is a short-form video social network and entertainment app with more than two million users, built around fanC, a cryptocurrency token that rewards creators and viewers.

What I worked on

  • A leading member of the four-person iOS team, owning about half of the app's features, including some of the most technically demanding.
  • Camera, recording and effects for short-form video with AVKit and ARKit.
  • Notifications, deep and universal links, and sign-in with Email, Phone, Apple, Google, Kakao and LINE.

Stack

Swift, Objective-C, UIKit, AVKit, ARKit, RxSwift, Firebase.
