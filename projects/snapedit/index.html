SnapEdit is SilverAI's AI photo editor: one-touch object removal, image enhancement, retouching and beauty tools, then frames, stickers, text and effects to finish a photo or a video. It holds a 4.8 rating from more than 14,000 reviews on the App Store.

What I work on

  • The iOS app since May 2024 — more than 500 commits across editing features, the camera and the effect pipeline.
  • Rendering effects and adjustments on device with Core Image and Metal, through the shared SnapStudio SDK and the cross-platform SnapEngine core.
  • Moving logic out of the app target into a tested Swift package, and the nightly test and TestFlight pipelines that ship it.
