SnapEngine

The cross-platform C++ core behind SnapEdit on iOS and Android.

Role

iOS engineer at SilverAI

Period

09/2026 – present

Platform

iOS, macOS, Android

Source

Private · SilverAI

Stack

  • Lua

SnapEngine holds the rules both SnapEdit apps have to agree on, written once instead of twice. One interface definition generates a C++ API, a pure-C bridge with a Swift facade, and Kotlin bindings over JNI. C++ decides what to do; each platform keeps what to do it with — Core Image and Metal on Apple, GLES on Android.

What it carries

  • Effects as portable bundles: a scene, GLSL passes, scripts and a declared parameter table.
  • A Lua runtime that animates presets identically on both operating systems.
  • A Swift package for iOS and macOS and an AAR for Android, neither needing CMake or the NDK to consume.
