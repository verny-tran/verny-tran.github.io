SnapCamera

The camera lab as a package: film, RAW and Log colour pipelines.

Platform

iOS 15+, macOS 14+

Private · SilverAI

Overview

SnapCamera is the colour science RollieCam and SnapEdit render with. A preset is a graph: each look carries a develop graph — decode, optics, colour, the shooter's adjustments — and the camera a skin graph that lays the looks into the export and draws over them.

What it carries

  • Forty-four render passes, from RAW decode, curves and LUTs to halation, grain and frames.
  • The .snapcamera preset format and the capture session every shooting app links.
  • Developing recorded video through the same graph as stills.

Stack

Swift, Core Image, Metal, AVFoundation.
