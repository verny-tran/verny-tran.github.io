SnapBaby

Finds your baby's photos with family and turns them into pictures and short videos.

Role

iOS engineer at SilverAI

Period

12/2024 – present

Platform

iOS 15.4+

Links

  • App Store

Overview

SnapBaby scans the photo library for pictures of a baby with parents and family, organises them, and turns them into milestone pictures and short videos with creative templates — less time managing photos, more time with them.

What I work on

  • Detecting and grouping the baby and family members on device, with Vision and Core ML over PhotoKit.
  • Template rendering for pictures and video with AVFoundation.

Stack

Swift, Vision, Core ML, PhotoKit, AVFoundation.
