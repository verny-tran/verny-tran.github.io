RollieCam

A pocket film camera: live film looks, plus RAW and Log capture.

Overview

RollieCam captures photos and video and develops them through faithful film, RAW and Log pipelines, so the analog look is in the viewfinder while you shoot rather than added afterwards. What you preview is what you export.

What I work on

  • The capture app, built on the SnapCamera package — its film, RAW and Log colour pipelines and the camera skins.
  • Live preview and video developing through the same graph the stills use.

Stack

Swift, AVFoundation, Core Image, Metal, SwiftUI.
