AttendanceKit

Automatic attendance with face recognition, UHF RFID and NFC.

Role

Period

2022 – 2024

Platform

iOS, macOS

Links

  • GitHub
  • FDSE 2022

Overview

A set of role-based iOS and macOS apps that check students' attendance in offline classes automatically, combining real-time UHF RFID and NFC with face recognition on the phone's camera. Students are notified and confirm in real time; instructors monitor attendance and get reports on learning status. The apps run at the campus of VNU-HCM International University.

Contributions

  • A custom admin module over Firebase Authentication, letting an institution create accounts for its students.
  • FaceNet, trained in TensorFlow and converted to Core ML, recognising students from five-second videos in real time on device.
  • A comparison of native frameworks, web APIs, model wrapping and model converting for face recognition on mobile — the subject of the second paper.

The project was funded by VNU-HCM under grants C2022-28-10 and B2023-30-05, and became my BSc thesis, defended in March 2024. Advised by Dr. Tu-Nga Ly, School of Computer Science and Engineering.

Stack

Swift, SwiftUI, Core ML, Vision, Core NFC, Firebase.
