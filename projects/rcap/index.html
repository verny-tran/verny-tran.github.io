RCAP

Finds the trips in your photo library and draws each one as a printable map.

Role

iOS engineer at SilverAI

Period

08/2026 – present

Platform

iOS 18+

Links

  • App Store

Overview

RCAP reads the dates and coordinates your camera already saved, works out where one trip ended and the next began, and assembles the route — the days, the stops, the distance — into a map worth printing. No tracker, no journal, and the photos never leave the device.

What I work on

  • The trip-detection engine, a Swift package tested on macOS without a simulator.
  • Five print layouts — Sheet, Trek, Plain, Pin and Text — and a passport of every country visited.

Stack

Swift, SwiftUI, PhotoKit, MapKit, Core Graphics.
